# Xue2AI

**Learn Mandarin Chinese with an AI that adapts to your level, your memory and your mistakes.**

---

## ✨ Features

- ✅ **AI teacher (AEAI)** — a conversational tutor that builds your profile, tracks your progress and adapts to your mistakes
- ✅ **Spaced repetition (FSRS)** — review decks optimized by a spaced-repetition algorithm
- ✅ **Pinyin & tones** — learn tones with colorized pinyin
- ✅ **Compare & introspection** — compare your pronunciation and analyze your errors
- ✅ **No account** — no sign-up, no login, your data stays on your computer
- ✅ **Bring your own services** — plug your own API keys (LLM, TTS, STT, images) in the settings

## ❓ FAQ

**Is the app free?**
Yes — the app is free to download and use.

**Is my data sent anywhere?**
No. Everything stays on your computer; only your API keys call the services you configured.

**How do I report a bug?**
Open an [issue](https://github.com/PapyNovator/xue2ai/issues).
